Quick presets

Each button below sets all the checkboxes for you - review and click "Save mute policy" after picking one if you want to tweak anything further.

What should the Muted role block?

Checked options are denied for members who have the Muted role. These settings are pushed to every existing channel a few seconds after you save (and again whenever /moderation mute is used).

Removes every removable role a member has when they're muted (booster/bot-managed roles and anything above the bot's own role are left alone since the bot can't touch those anyway), then adds them back automatically when the mute ends - whether that's the timer running out or /moderation unmute/the dashboard's Unmute. This stops a member from leaning on some other role's permissions to get around the Muted role.

Discord Administrators and your configured moderation exemptions are still subject to your normal server rules; the Muted role only controls the permissions listed above.

Vote Kick

Let members vote to kick someone. Votes survive a bot restart. This does not bypass the bot's normal Discord role hierarchy: the bot still must be able to kick the target.
