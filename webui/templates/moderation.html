Choose an existing role or let the bot create/reuse a Muted role. The same configuration is used by /mute in Discord and by the dashboard.

Quick presets

Each button below sets all the checkboxes for you - review and click "Save mute policy" after picking one if you want to tweak anything further.

What should the Muted role block?

Checked options are denied for members who have the Muted role. These settings are applied to existing channels when you save and again when /mute is used.

Discord Administrators and your configured moderation exemptions are still subject to your normal server rules; the Muted role only controls the permissions listed above.

Moderation tools

Use the Discord commands for immediate actions such as /warn, /warnings, /clearwarns, /mute, /unmute, /kick, /ban, /tempban, and /tempnick.

The dashboard is for configuration and moderation history; the bot remains the authority for live Discord actions.
