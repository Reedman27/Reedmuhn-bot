What gets logged?

  • Messages: edits, deletes, attachments, and bulk deletes.
  • Members: joins, leaves, nickname changes, and role changes.
  • Moderation: bans, unbans, kicks, and timeouts, with audit-log moderator/reason information when Discord provides it.
  • Server: channel, role, and server configuration changes.
  • Voice: joins, leaves, and moves between voice channels.

The bot falls back gracefully when it cannot read the audit log or send to a configured logging channel.
