Ticket panel

Posts a persistent "Open a Ticket" button in one channel - this is the "ticket tool" replacement: members click the button instead of needing to know /ticket. Clicking it always opens a brand new private channel just for them (the same category/support-role settings above apply), so there's no need to lock down who can type in the panel channel itself - everyone can see the button, but only the person who clicks it ever gets into the ticket it creates.
