Your staff access

  3. Reports, Tickets, Modmail, Suggestions: triage what members send in.

Get running in 10 minutes

  1. Pick who sees this dashboard. Dashboard Access: leave it open to Administrators, or narrow it to just you. Add moderators as Staff.
  2. Turn on logging. Logging → auto-create channels so you can see what happens.
  3. Protect the server. AutoMod, Raid Detection and Anti-Nuke.
  4. Welcome people. Welcome & Roles and Verification.
  5. Add what you want. Tickets, reaction roles, leveling, music... everything else is optional and lives in the menu on the left.
