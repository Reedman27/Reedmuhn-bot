🚨 Raid Detection

Watches how fast members are joining and reacts once it crosses a threshold within a short window - the same kind of burst detector as Anti-Nuke, but keyed on joins instead of audit-log actions.

Alert only: just posts a warning, doesn't touch anyone. Auto-kick: while raid mode is active, kicks any new joiner whose account is younger than the cutoff below. Lock all channels: immediately locks every text channel the same way the Emergency page's Lockdown button does - you'll need to unlock manually once things settle.

Raid mode stays active for the cooldown period after the last flagged join, so a raid that keeps trickling in doesn't get treated as "over" too early.
